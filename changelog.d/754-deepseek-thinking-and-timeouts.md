Fixed

* **DeepSeek's thinking mode is chosen per call, an empty answer is a named error, and every timeout derives from one setting (issue #754).** The default team (DeepSeek, #739, #748) ran every call in DeepSeek's default thinking mode, so the planner, the QA review and the engram extraction spent their `max_tokens` reasoning and got an empty answer, and slow calls outlived gunicorn's 120 s worker timeout while DeepSeek kept the connection alive with empty lines. Every call now sends `thinking: {"type": "enabled" | "disabled"}` (https://api-docs.deepseek.com/guides/thinking_mode) from one policy, `services/models/thinking.py`: off for calls whose answer is parsed (`LLMProxyClient.structured`, the EngramProcessor), on for the free-form calls of PO, Architect, SecOps, the coding roles and Designer with `LLM_THINKING_RESERVE_TOKENS` (16384) added to `max_tokens`, and `LLM_THINKING=on|off` forces every call; the proxy sends the field to DeepSeek only and sets `disabled` when a request has none. A 2xx answer with no text and no tool call is now a `502` with `reason` `empty_content_reasoning_exhausted` (cut at `finish_reason: length` after `reasoning_content` used the budget) or `empty_content`, never an empty success and never the reasoning as the answer; a structured call that exhausted its budget reasoning is retried once with thinking off (`thinking_retry` on the answer). `LLM_UPSTREAM_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` defaults to 300 (was 110) and is enforced as a deadline on the whole body (`reason: upstream_timeout`); gunicorn reads its settings from `llm-proxy/gunicorn.conf.py` (gthread workers, `LLM_PROXY_WORKERS` x `LLM_PROXY_THREADS`, worker timeout the budget plus 30 s) and the backend waits the budget plus 15 s, with compose passing the setting to both; a backend read timeout (`proxy_read_timeout`) or a dropped connection (`proxy_connection_lost`) is a named error and is no longer sent again. The sprint planner logs why it used its deterministic plan, and its `planner_fallback` (issue #753) now carries the named reason as observed: a `model_error` detail starts with `empty_content_reasoning_exhausted`, `empty_content` (an empty 2xx answer is no longer parsed as `unparseable`), `upstream_timeout`, `proxy_read_timeout` or `proxy_connection_lost`, an `unparseable` detail gives the answer's length, and the foundation plan records its fallback the same way. Rebuild the proxy image (`docker compose up -d --build llm-proxy`): it has new modules.
