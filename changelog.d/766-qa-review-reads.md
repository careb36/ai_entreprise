* **QA reads the delivery before its verdict (issue #766).** The review turn saw the branch diff, the agent's message and the test output only in excerpts, and reviews said they stopped where it mattered or could not confirm a claim. Where the task's delivery was recorded and the daemon answers, QA now gets the read-only workspace tools (`list`, `read`, `search`, `find_file`; never `edit` or `run_tests`) on the folder the verification recorded, through the Developer's tool loop, for `QA_REVIEW_TOOL_ROUNDS` rounds (default 3). The verdict keeps the same messages, client and parser; an answer without a verdict falls back to today's single call, as does a review with no recorded delivery or no daemon. Each reading round is a priced `qa_review_read` audit turn outside the QA rate, and every call's observation goes on the verdict's turn (`tool_calls`).
