Added

* **Personal notifications: one row per receiver, snooze and preferences (issue #683).** Each person now has their own notices, one `personal_notifications` row per receiver and company (migration `0057`) holding the kind, a reference to the subject and the integer ids its link needs, never the subject's text (GDPR data minimisation and purpose limitation). The bell in the top bar counts the unread ones and `/notifications` lists, marks read and snoozes them (WCAG 2.2 AA), with a preference per kind: shown in the app, and copied by email only when `SMTP_HOST` is set, so nothing waits on mail without it. Security notices cannot be muted in the app and are snoozed for 24 hours at most (ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.5.24–A.5.26). Wired producers: the SSO break-glass team exclusion and its lifting (to the other Owners, besides the NATS `company.owner_notice`), an SSO domain unverified by a re-check (to every Owner; `sso_domains.notify_lapsed` is the hook for the 14-day unconfirmed lapse of #684), a risk hold waiting for a person to accept it and a sprint integration waiting for a person (to the Managers and Owners). A person reads only their own rows (`GET/POST /v1/notifications…`, declared in `docs/architecture/authorization.json`); the janitor deletes them after `RETENTION_NOTIFICATIONS_DAYS` (90, never unbounded) and at once for a person who left the company. Design taken from Plane's notification model, no code.
