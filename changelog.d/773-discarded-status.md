Added

* **A person can discard a task, and comment on it from its page (issue #773).** A sixth status, `discarded`, set only by a Manager or Owner with the reason as the comment, on a task in To do or Blocked. It is settled for the sprint's close, never dispatched or carried over, holds a task that depends on it for a person, and reopens when moved to To do; the history checker refuses one without a person's reason. The task page has a Comments panel with Discard and Reopen; the board shows a Discarded column only when it has tasks.
