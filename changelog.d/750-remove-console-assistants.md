Removed

* **Console assistants: the team gives the second opinion (issue #750).** Before a task, the task worker asked any GitHub Copilot CLI or Claude CLI the workspace daemon found on its computer for advice, on by default (`CONSOLE_ASSISTANTS_ENABLED=true`): the CLI ran under the account signed in there, billed to it, and received the task and the repository's context, with no decision recorded (a sprint log showed "Claude CLI contributed planning and architecture advice", $0.55, on a team the owner had made DeepSeek only, #739, #748). That is removed, not put behind a setting: GDPR art. 25 and 28, ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.5.19 to A.5.23 and NIST SP 800-53 AC-20 require a recorded choice before a third party receives a company's context, and the team already gives that advice on its own models. Gone: `services/producers/console_assistants.py`, `console_commands.py`, `console_capabilities.py` and `assistant_sandbox.py`, the startup probe of `workspace.capabilities`, the `assistant.telemetry` Glassroom events and their badges and summary panel, the board's console-advice note, the prompt's `console_assistant_notes` section, and `console_advice` in `GET /v1/sprints` with its card on the Sprints page. The Architect's consultation, on the Architect's role default model (DeepSeek Pro unless the company or `AGENT_MODEL_ARCHITECT` chose another), now also covers design-sensitive, high-priority or reworked tasks of QA, PO and ScrumMaster, the roles the CLI used to advise besides the coding ones (never the Architect's own tasks). The settings `CONSOLE_ASSISTANTS_ENABLED`, `CONSOLE_ASSISTANT_TIMEOUT_SECONDS`, `CONSOLE_ASSISTANT_STRATEGY`, `CONSOLE_ASSISTANT_ALLOWED_ROLES`, `COPILOT_CLI_COMMAND_TEMPLATE`, `CLAUDE_CLI_COMMAND_TEMPLATE`, `CLAUDE_CLI_ALLOWED_TOOLS`, `CLAUDE_CLI_APPEND_SYSTEM_PROMPT` and `CLAUDE_CLI_HIGH_VALUE_ROLES` do nothing; a `.env` that still sets one loads, and the backend names each at startup (`is set but no longer does anything`). No migration. Unchanged: the console producers (a terminal coding agent a Manager names for a role, `PUT /v1/companies/{id}/console_producers`), which are never on by default or by detection; their cooldowns moved to `services/producers/cli_cooldowns.py`, and `CLAUDE_CLI_DEFAULT_COOLDOWN_MINUTES` still applies to them. The workspace daemon still answers `workspace.capabilities` and accepts the sandbox purpose `assistant` for older backends.
