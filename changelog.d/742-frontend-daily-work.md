Added

* **Daily-work settings and records in the web app (`docs/archive/design/FRONTEND_GAPS.md`, P2).** Two new company pages, readable by every member and changed by a Manager: **Project settings** (`/company/project`: how a sprint's team is chosen, `…/team_selection`, and the stack profile's overrides of the detection, each command left detected, set or turned off, the folders' own profiles kept as stored, `…/stack_profile`) and **Notifications** (`/company/notifications`: the notification webhook, https only, a write-only signing secret of at least 16 characters, the events, a test ping and a confirmed delete, `…/webhook`). An approved specification without an architecture of its own offers a Manager the next version of the company's architecture (`POST …/architecture/revisions`, or a link to the open draft); an architecture's decisions download as ADR files (`GET /v1/architectures/{id}/adrs`); a documentation pack's signed manifest downloads (`GET /v1/documentation/{id}/manifest`); and the sprint's Overview lists its task relations with their numbers (`GET /v1/sprints/{id}/relations`).
