* **Tools follow the step, the role only narrows (issue #766).** Which tool families a turn is offered is now decided by its step (`services/contracts/tool_steps.py`): an execution turn of any role reads, writes where edits may go, runs the folder's tests and committed scripts, and researches (registries, advisories, allowed hosts), so a TechWriter, SecOps, Architect or PO executing a task gets the workspace tools; a brief, a review, a planning turn or the ScrumMaster's decision only reads. A role's deny set (empty by default) can drop families within its step and never add one. The run's `tools_offered` records the step and its families. The PO's sprint planning and the ScrumMaster's decision on a blocked task read the project through the read-only toolbox (`PLANNING_TOOL_ROUNDS`, `SCRUM_TRIAGE_TOOL_ROUNDS`), each call an observation on the turn, and fall back to one plain call without a daemon or a project path. The planner's reassignment of runnable work away from a role without tools is now a fallback for a role that denies them.
