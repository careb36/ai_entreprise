Changed

* **Every reading step gets the tools its step allows, and documentation tasks write documentation only (issue #766).** The read-only toolboxes of the QA review, the Architect's brief, the PO's sprint plan and the ScrumMaster's triage now offer `workspace__git` (with `blame`) and `workspace__read_document` when every daemon announces them (`services/tools/workspace_reads.py`). The Architect's brief gets the research tools (`research__package`, `research__advisories`, a fetch of the allowed hosts) beside its reads, as its step allows. The record tools `board__task` and `board__evidence` go beside the ScrumMaster's reads, scoped to the blocked task's sprint, and beside the PO's planning reads, scoped to the company's board; their readers moved to `services/tasks/task_records.py`. None of these is offered to a turn without its reads. Where a turn writes is now part of the step table (`contracts/tool_steps.write_scope`): the `documentation` step, and the TechWriter in any step that writes, write documentation only; a task whose kind is `documentation` is executed as that step. The edit tools refuse other paths as before, and the files an answer delivers in its own blocks follow the same rule: a non-documentation path is not delivered, the review payload says so and the run's evidence records `docs_write` with the reason. `workspace__lint` is family X: offered only where the step allows runs.
