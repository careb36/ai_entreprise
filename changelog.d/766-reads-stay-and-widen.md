Changed

* **Reads stay whole in the tool history and show as many lines as fit (issue #766).** An audit of 1063 Developer reads found 16% re-reading the exact window the history had summarized three rounds earlier, and 601 stopping at the 100-line default (a 642-line file was never read past line 500). An old `workspace__read` now stays whole while it is still the latest view of its lines (no later edit, move or delete of the file, no later read covering them) and all tool results fit `TOOL_HISTORY_MAX_CHARS` (default 120000 characters); list, search and test outputs are summarized first, then the oldest reads, each line naming the path, the lines and the call that shows them again. A read without `lines` asks the daemon for 400 lines and shows as many whole lines as fit the model's result budget (`result_max_chars`, 16000 for DeepSeek: about 400 short lines instead of 100); the observation's `last_line` is the last line shown. A daemon that predates `lines` keeps answering 100; `READ_MAX_BYTES` is unchanged.
