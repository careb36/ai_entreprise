Added

* **Owner actions to land a branch, push main to GitHub and close an issue, each with its own confirmation (issue #701).** Three entries join the owner actions catalogue (`backend/app/services/management/owner_actions.py`), with their facts and previews in the new `owner_git_actions.py` and their jobs in the new `owner_git_runner.py`. `land_branch` runs `scripts/land.sh <branch>` on the daemon's computer through the new `workspace.self_host.land` subject (`vscode-daemon/self_host_git.py`), for a branch picked from the daemon's own list; the preview shows the commits, the diffstat and the protected paths, and a protected land needs a fresh password (or an SSO sign-in under five minutes, `backend/app/step_up.py`) before `LAND_OWNER=1` is set for that run only. It is refused while a sprint is active or a self-hosting run is in flight, never pushes, and records the daemon's `main` before and after apart from land.sh's verification record and its `check.sh full` summary. `push_main` fast-forwards `origin`'s `main` to the previewed commit with land.sh's notes through `workspace.self_host.push`, with the daemon machine's own git credentials, never forced; the preview lists the commits that become public under a PUBLIC warning, and a push that is not a fast-forward is refused. `close_issue` picks an open issue through the GitHub adapter (`backend/app/services/producers/github_adapter.py`: `list_open_issues`, `comment_on_issue`, `close_issue`), prefills the comment from the fixing commit and whether `origin` has it, posts exactly the text confirmed, and is refused when the fix is not on `origin` unless the Owner overrides it. The read-only `workspace.self_host.git` subject gives the previews; every preview's digest covers what it observed. `GET /v1/owner_actions/{name}/options` lists the branches or issues to pick from. The Owner actions page shows the three with their dialogs (`frontend/src/pages/ownerActions/RepoActionCard.tsx`, `RepoConfirmDialog.tsx`). Daemon setting: `FABRIQUE_SELF_HOST_LAND_TIMEOUT`.
