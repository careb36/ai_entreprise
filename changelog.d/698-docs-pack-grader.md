Added

* **A grader for documentation packs (issue #698).** `scripts/bench/docs_pack_grade.py` compares a pack with expectations a person wrote for a reference specification (`la-fabrique.docs-pack-expectations/1`): the documents present, headings and texts in them, documents with no section left without a record, the requirements the traceability matrix lists and whether each has a task, a verified delivery and an integration, and the result each named check computes. It writes a card (`la-fabrique.docs-pack-grade/1`) with every item as pass, fail or unmeasured, the score, the verdict and its own sha256 of the files it read. To grade without parsing Markdown, the traceability matrix and the checks document now carry their records as data (`data.trace`, `data.checks`); a pack compiled before that is unmeasured, never failed. No expectations are committed: they are a person's to write, under `bench/docs_packs/`.
