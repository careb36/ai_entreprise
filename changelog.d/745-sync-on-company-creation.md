* **New company: bring the local copy up to date with its remote before the first sprint (issue #745).** When the chosen folder is a git repository, the New company form shows what the workspace daemon read locally: the remote URL without credentials, the current and base branch, and whether the checkout is clean; "Check remote" fetches the base's one branch and says how far it is behind or ahead, and nothing contacts the remote before it is pressed. The Owner chooses the company's forge policy there (`none`, `fetch` or `publish`, never above `FORGE_POLICY_MAX`, now also in `GET /v1/capabilities`), `fetch` by default only when the folder has a remote, audited as `forge_policy_changed` with `at: creation` even when it is `none`. With "Bring my local copy up to date" ticked under `fetch` or above, the daemon fast-forwards the local base to its remote before the folder is read, through the new `workspace.git.sync_base` subject (`vscode-daemon/git_sync.py`, enforced by `forge_gate`): `git update-ref` with the old value, or `git merge --ff-only` in the checkout that has the base; no merge commit, rebase or reset. A diverged, dirty, mid-rebase or unreachable repository is refused with its reason, refs and index unchanged, and the company is created either way; what the daemon observed (remote, base, old and new commit, counts, outcome) is recorded on the company as `base_sync_observed`. The same action is on the company's Git settings and on the board of a sprint that has not started, when the local base is behind (`POST /v1/companies/{id}/git/sync_base`, Manager). `workspace.git.base_status` also reports `current_branch`, `clean`, `operation_in_progress` and `remote_url`, read locally (`POST /v1/workspace/git_check`).
