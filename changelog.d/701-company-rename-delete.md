* **An Owner can rename the company or delete it from the app.** The company settings page has a Company panel: rename it, or delete it after seeing how many rows each table holds and typing the name back. Deleting removes every row the company holds, its audit chain included, in one transaction, and is refused while a sprint or an owner action runs; the deployment's audit row records who deleted what. Only a signed-in Owner can delete, never an API token. The project folder and its branches stay on the computer.
