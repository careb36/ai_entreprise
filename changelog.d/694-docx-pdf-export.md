* **The documentation pack downloads as DOCX and as PDF/A-3b declaring PDF/UA-1, each with a signed manifest (issue #694).** `GET /v1/documentation/{id}/export/{docx|pdf}` (Viewer) renders the stored pack, never request input, through our own parser of the pack's Markdown subset: headings, nested lists, pipe tables, the claim marks, person sections and statements as quotes, citations and sources as they are, and each Mermaid diagram as its source under a label. DOCX uses python-docx with Word's built-in styles; PDF uses WeasyPrint, tagged, with embedded DejaVu fonts, the sRGB output intent and both declarations in its XMP. A cover page names the version, the status and the pack manifest's digest. The bytes are reproducible, so `GET .../export/{fmt}/manifest` lists the export's sha256 beside the pack's files, signed like the pack's manifest. veraPDF 1.30.4 passed the output for PDF/A-3b and PDF/UA-1 when it was built; CI checks the declarations and structure, and runs veraPDF only where it is installed. Without Pango the backend starts and the PDF route answers 503 with the reason (`GET /v1/documentation/exports`). The backend image gains Pango and the DejaVu fonts (about +100 MB, estimated); Pillow and Pyphen are listed as licence exceptions pending the owner's decision. The Documentation page has DOCX and PDF/A buttons with their manifests, in English and Spanish. Organisation templates stay designed, not built.
